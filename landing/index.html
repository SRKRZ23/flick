Privacy

Your files never leave your Mac.

Online converters make you upload a copy of your file to a server you don't control — private documents, photos, and their metadata included. Flick doesn't have a server to upload to.

↑

Uploading is the risk

Once your file leaves your Mac, you have to trust how a stranger stores, processes and deletes it.

⌂

Flick works locally

Every conversion runs on your own machine. No account, no telemetry, no network.

Free, and open source.

Flick is MIT-licensed. Read the code, build it yourself, or send a pull request. No paywall, no "pro" tier.
